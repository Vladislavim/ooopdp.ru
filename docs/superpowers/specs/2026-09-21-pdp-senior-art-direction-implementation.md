# PDP senior art direction implementation

## Approved direction

Implement the recommendations from `output/art-direction-research/PDP-SENIOR-ART-DIRECTION-REPORT.md` in this order:

1. Service Detail — flexible chapter rhythm, with orange used as one authored accent rather than every section background.
2. Cases — curated sequence for the strongest projects; preserve large project focus and the archive.
3. Case Detail — evidence-led chapters that connect role, facts, media and documents.
4. Services Hub — live expertise index with one visual stage; preserve industry proof and project material.
5. Articles — editorial front page with one dominant story and a quieter archive.
6. Documents — trust/evidence presentation led by the real presentation and supporting documents.
7. About — thesis-led company story with facts embedded in the narrative.

## Constraints

- Desktop Home is frozen and must remain visually unchanged.
- Contacts is read-only unless QA reveals a functional or responsive defect.
- Preserve all business claims, protected facts, routes, metadata and SEO architecture.
- Work through source files and existing CSS owners; generated HTML is build output.
- Preserve strong photography, the PDP palette, structural typography, sharp geometry and the shared shell.
- Do not force all page families into one composition.

## Responsive and validation

After each family, build the generated pages and compare before/after at 1440, 768 and 390 pixels. Check composition, crop, hierarchy, interaction, focus, overflow, console errors and asset failures. Stop polishing after one correction pass unless a functional defect remains.

## Definition of done

The seven page families are visibly stronger and more content-specific, while desktop Home and Contacts remain unchanged. Build reports zero output differences after write, browser QA passes at all three target widths, and the final report distinguishes rebuilds from polish and preserved areas.
